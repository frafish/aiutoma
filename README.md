# Aiutoma for WordPress

**Aiutoma** is the ultimate agentic assistant designed to bring true AI automation to your WordPress and WooCommerce ecosystem. Transform your website into an intelligent, self-managing platform that generates stunning content and connects seamlessly to external models.

## 💎 The 100% Native WordPress Experience

Unlike generic wrapper plugins, Aiutoma is engineered specifically for the WordPress ecosystem:
- **WordPress 7.0 AI API Ready:** Built directly on top of the futuristic native WordPress Core AI Connectors and Abilities API. It doesn't hack its way into your site; it utilizes standardized, official protocols.
- **Seamless Native UI/UX:** Forget clunky, bloated third-party interfaces. Aiutoma's dashboard, settings, and editor panels are meticulously crafted to blend perfectly into the classic WordPress UI mood. It feels like a feature WordPress shipped out of the box.
- **Strict Standards Compliance:** Written with clean, secure, and modern PHP 8.1 standards. No heavy external frameworks, no performance dragging. Just perfectly compliant, highly optimized code.
- **EU AI Act & GDPR Ready by Design:** Built from the ground up to respect European regulations (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). Meets Article 50 transparency requirements out of the box with persistent AI badges, accuracy disclaimers, clear human operator handovers, and Privacy Policy links.

## 🚀 The Complete AI Suite

### 1. 🦸‍♂️ AI Playground (Command Center)
Your extremely powerful, yet perfectly safe, sandboxed command center. Instruct the AI to manage WordPress content, options, taxonomies, and execute safe DB queries. Everything is backed up automatically with instant 1-click rollbacks.

### 2. 🪄 Gutenberg Editor Agent
Meet your new co-writer. A smart, floating chatbot lives right inside your block editor. Type `/prompt` anywhere to visually generate Gutenberg blocks, rewrite entire paragraphs, or autonomously create and attach AI-generated featured images directly to your media library.

### 3. 🆓 Free-Tier Providers Included
Start automating at absolute zero cost. Aiutoma integrates natively with top AI providers that offer generous free tiers (like Google Gemini and HuggingFace). Experience enterprise-grade AI capabilities without worrying about monthly API bills.

### 4. 🌍 WPML Auto-Translation
Take your business global effortlessly. With native WPML integration, Aiutoma can automatically detect, translate, and sync your content across multiple languages, keeping your international SEO flawless and up-to-date.

### 5. 📡 LLMs.txt & Markdown Endpoints (AI-Ready SEO)
Prepare your website for the next generation of search engines. Aiutoma automatically exposes ultra-clean `.md` endpoints for your public content, mapping your entire site architecture via an autogenerated `/llms.txt` file for flawless ingestion by ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity crawlers.

### 6. 🔗 MCP & OpenAPI Integrations
Take remote control of your WordPress site. Connect Claude Desktop (via the Model Context Protocol) or Custom GPTs (via OpenAPI Custom Actions) to manage your site, read data, and execute tasks securely from your favorite AI clients.

### 7. 🔔 AI Webhook Receiver
Keep track of everything. Provide external AI agents, Zapier flows, or background cron tasks with a secure REST endpoint that logs their automated actions directly into your native WordPress Comments (cleanly attached to specific post IDs).

### 8. 🧠 RAG Knowledge Base
Give your AI true, deep context. Automatically sync your posts, WooCommerce products, and user data into a high-performance, local SQLite vector database. Your AI will always know your exact inventory and data.

### 9. 💬 Frontend Chatbot (EU AI Act Ready)
Empower your visitors with total legal confidence. Deploy a sleek, lightning-fast frontend chat widget powered by your RAG embeddings to provide instant customer support, smart WooCommerce recommendations, and guided user experiences:
- **EU AI Act (Art. 50) Transparency:** Built-in permanent AI indicator badges and accuracy disclaimers ensure users always know they are chatting with an AI system.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Handover:** Seamlessly take over live chats from the admin dashboard with real-time system notices informing visitors when an operator joins or leaves.
- **Privacy & GDPR Controls:** Session tracking controls, customizable consent checkboxes, and automated Privacy Policy page integration.

## 🇪🇺 EU AI Act Compliance (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)
Aiutoma is engineered to keep your website legally compliant as an AI deployer under European law:
- **Risk Classification:** Aiutoma operates as a **Limited Risk** AI system. It does not engage in prohibited practices (Art. 5) or high-risk decision-making (Art. 6 / Annex III).
- **Article 50 Transparency Obligations:** Natural persons are clearly and continuously informed that they are interacting with an AI system through visible header badges, explicit initial greetings, and persistent accuracy disclaimers.
- **Human Editorial Oversight:** Backend generation tools (Gutenberg agent, SEO, and Playground) require explicit human review and publication, fulfilling the oversight standards of Art. 50(4).

## 🛠️ Setup & Requirements
- **Requires:** WordPress 6.0+, PHP 8.1+
- **API Keys:** Uses native WP Core AI Connectors. Just plug in your keys and watch the magic happen.

## 🌐 External Services

This plugin connects to external third-party services to provide artificial intelligence completions, embeddings generation, anti-bot verification, image creation, content translation, and instant messaging functionality. All external connections are made directly from your WordPress server to the respective service endpoints. No personal data is sent without explicit user or administrator action.

### 1. Cohere
- **Service & Purpose:** Connects to the Cohere API (`api.cohere.ai`, `dashboard.cohere.com`) for text completions and embeddings when configured by the administrator.
- **Data Sent & When:** Prompt text, chat history, and model parameters sent upon user-initiated AI queries.
- **Provider:** Cohere Inc.
- **Terms & Privacy:** [terms of use](https://cohere.com/terms-of-use) | [privacy policy](https://cohere.com/privacy)

### 2. Groq
- **Service & Purpose:** Connects to the GroqCloud API (`api.groq.com`, `console.groq.com`) for high-speed LLM inference when configured with a Groq API key.
- **Data Sent & When:** Prompt text, system instructions, and chat messages sent upon executing queries.
- **Provider:** Groq, Inc.
- **Terms & Privacy:** [terms of use](https://groq.com/terms-of-use/) | [privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/)

### 3. Hugging Face

- **Provider:** Hugging Face, Inc.
- **Terms & Privacy:** [terms of service](https://huggingface.co/terms-of-service) | [privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy)
